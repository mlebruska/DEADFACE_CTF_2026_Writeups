# Car Crash

## Objective

Identify a company DEADFACE is targeting based on an image of a crashed truck (truck_crash.png)

## The Solve

Zooming into the image, I see that the side of the truck says

```
USDOT: 080806
```

I wasn't entirely sure what this meant, but I searched "usdot number lookup" and found the website https://motus.dot.gov/public/search

I plugged the number in and found that 080806 corresponds to J B HUNT TRANSPORT INC.

A quick search for the company name reveals that it was founded August 10th, 1961 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._B._Hunt)

Thus, the flag is

```
deadface{08_10_1961}
```
